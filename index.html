  • MCP ORDER Aspirin administration; Nitroglycerin administration; accessing the GMVEMSC Drug Bag (2008.2 Consult, PDF p.59; 8004 and 8034 Medical Control: EMTs Yes).
  • MAY EMT: apply O2 as appropriate and per saturation thresholds; acquire supine {12 Lead EKG} prior to moving the patient; {transmit 12 Lead EKG} with two identifiers to MCP; consult MCP for destination (2008.2 EMR/EMT blocks).
  • MAY EMT may assist the advanced provider, under direct supervision of the AEMT or Paramedic, with: IV access; IV fluid administration; saline locks; placement of 4 and/or {12/15 Lead EKG}; accessing the GMVEMSC Drug Bag to locate drugs and/or assemble pre-jects (1005.3, PDF p.21).
